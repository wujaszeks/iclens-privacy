Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the IC Lens mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created and maintained as a commercial service.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and processes information to provide, maintain, and improve the service. This includes:

  • Camera Access: The Application requires permission to use your device's camera to scan components, perform optical character recognition (OCR), and capture images for analysis. Captured images are processed to extract text.
  • Local Storage: Scanned history, favorites, and user settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Network Data: The Application communicates with servers over the internet to perform component searches and retrieve datasheet information.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing Client (for managing premium subscriptions)
  • Google Gemini API / AI Services (for datasheet and PDF link analysis)

3. Data Retention

Any scan history or favorite components you save are kept locally on your device. We do not host databases containing your personal images or private scan history on our servers. You can clear your local data at any time by clearing the Application's cache or uninstalling the Application.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our records.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@iclens.app.
